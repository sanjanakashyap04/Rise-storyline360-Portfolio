Leadership Readiness Pathway

A scenario-based learning experience designed to build foundational judgment skills for high-performing individual contributors preparing to step into leadership.

Learning Gap

Organizations often promote strong individual contributors into management without fully preparing them for the judgment required to lead people. The Leadership Readiness Pathway addresses that gap through realistic scenarios focused on coaching, delegation, conflict, and strategic decision-making.

Terminal Learning Objective

By the end of the Leadership Readiness Pathway, promotion-track individual contributors will be able to evaluate realistic people-leadership scenarios spanning coaching, delegation, conflict, and resourcing trade-offs and select the response that reflects sound managerial judgment, avoiding the failure patterns modelled at each module's decision points.

Project Overview

The Leadership Readiness Pathway is a portfolio project focused on developing practical people-leadership judgment before learners formally step into management.

Audience Promotion-track and high-potential individual contributors who are not yet in a formal leadership role.
Tools Articulate Rise 360 and Storyline 360
Learning Format Scenario-based learning, branching decisions, knowledge checks and consequence-based feedback.
Estimated Duration Approximately 20 minutes

Design Approach

The experience treats leadership as a judgment skill rather than a collection of facts to memorize.

Branching Decisions

Learners make decisions within realistic leadership situations rather than simply selecting answers to recall-based questions.

Consequence-Based Feedback

Choices lead to realistic consequences, allowing learners to see how different leadership responses can affect a situation.

Path-Dependent Interaction

The fully built module uses the learner's earlier decision path to inform what happens later in the scenario.

Personalised Reflection

The experience closes with a variable-driven personalised summary rather than a simple pass/fail score.

04 · Development

Story View: Branching Map

The Module 1 scenario has two decision points, each with three response paths, that converge on a reflection slide.

05 · Scenario Setup

Setting the Scene

A character-led slide places the learner in the role of a team lead who notices a strong performer behaving unusually.

06 · Scenario Interaction

Decision Point & Triggers

Learners choose between three realistic responses. Each choice stores the selection and a response pattern in Storyline variables.

07 · Personalised Reflection

Reflection Slide

Variables carry both decisions into a personalised debrief on the learner's tendencies, with a restart option that resets the choices.

08 · Branching Logic

Decision 2 Trigger Logic

Each option records the learner's choice and pattern, then jumps to its own consequence slide, creating the branching path.

09 · Knowledge Check

Feedback on an Incorrect Answer

Rather than only marking the answer wrong, the feedback explains why it falls short and prompts the learner to try again.

10 · Knowledge Check

Feedback on the Correct Answer

Correct-answer feedback reinforces the reasoning behind the best response, not just the fact that it was right.
