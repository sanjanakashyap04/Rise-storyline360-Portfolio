Instructional Design Portfolio Project

NexaTech Onboarding Pathway

A structured onboarding experience designed to help new employees understand the organisation, make sound workplace decisions, find the right resources, and take confident action.

Business Problem / Learning Gap

New employees receive a large amount of onboarding information from different sources, but information alone does not ensure confident workplace performance.

The NexaTech onboarding pathway addresses the gap between knowing information and being able to use it. The experience is designed to help new hires understand the organisation, identify the right resources and contacts, apply workplace expectations, and complete essential onboarding actions independently.

Terminal Learning Objective

By the end of the onboarding pathway, new employees will be able to navigate key organisational information, identify appropriate resources and contacts, apply workplace expectations to realistic situations, and translate their onboarding knowledge into concrete first-week and 30-day actions.

Project at a Glance

Audience
New employees across departments
Format
Structured onboarding eLearning
Development
Rise 360 + Storyline 360
Pathway
8-module onboarding experience

Learning Architecture

The pathway moves learners from organisational understanding toward realistic decisions, resource-finding, and concrete onboarding actions.

Module 1

Welcome & Orientation

Understand what onboarding covers and how to navigate the learning journey.

Module 2

Mission, Vision & Values

Connect NexaTech's purpose and values to workplace behaviour.

Module 3

Organisation & Roles

Identify teams, responsibilities, and who to approach for common needs.

Module 4

Policies & Expectations

Apply workplace and security expectations through realistic decision-making scenarios.

Module 5

Logistics

Locate essential practical information needed for day-to-day work.

Module 6

Culture & Ways of Working

Apply cultural expectations such as ownership and communication in a realistic workplace dilemma.

Module 7

Tools, Resources & Support

Locate the appropriate tool, resource, or contact for a given workplace need.

Module 8

Knowledge Check & Next Steps

Demonstrate understanding and translate learning into first-week and 30-day actions.

Featured Build: Module 4 — Policies & Expectations

The primary Storyline showcase uses decision-based scenarios to move learners beyond policy recall and into realistic workplace judgment.

Decision Point 01

Document-Sharing Scenario

Learners respond to a colleague requesting a client rollout document. Multiple response options lead to consequence-based feedback, allowing learners to see the implications of different approaches.

Decision Point 02

Embedded Guidance Lookup

Learners encounter a second sharing request and must first check the relevant guidance before responding.

Interaction Design

Branching + Consequence Feedback

Learner choices determine the feedback experience, creating a direct connection between the decision made and the workplace consequence.

Performance Support

Resource Lookup Within the Scenario

The scenario pairs branching with just-in-time performance support, reinforcing the expectation that employees should know where to find guidance rather than rely on memory alone.

Design & Development

Rise 360 is used for the core onboarding experience, providing a structured and accessible learning journey across the pathway. Storyline 360 is used selectively for higher-complexity decision-making moments where branching and consequence feedback add instructional value.

Module 4 serves as the primary fully developed Storyline showcase. The scenario-based interaction gives learners an opportunity to apply workplace expectations rather than simply recall policy information.

Design Approach

The pathway follows a progression from understanding to application. Learners first build organisational context, then work through realistic decisions, locate relevant resources, and finish by translating their learning into concrete onboarding actions.
