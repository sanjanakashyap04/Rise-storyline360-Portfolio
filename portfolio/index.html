What I Do

I combine my background in teaching and curriculum development with instructional design practices to create purposeful digital learning experiences.

Learning Experience Design

Designing learning journeys around clear objectives, learner needs, meaningful practice, and real world application.

Scenario Based eLearning

Creating realistic situations where learners practice judgment, make decisions, and experience the consequences of their choices.

Curriculum and Storyboard Design

Translating learning goals into structured curriculum maps, storyboards, interactions, and assessment opportunities.

Interactive Development

Building interactive learning experiences using Articulate Rise 360 and Storyline 360.

Design Approach

I design for what learners need to do, not just what they need to know. My approach combines the structure of curriculum design with the practical focus of workplace learning.

01

Start with the Learning Problem

Identify the performance need before deciding on the learning solution.

02

Design for Practice

Give learners opportunities to apply judgment, make decisions, and work through realistic situations.

03

Make Interaction Meaningful

Use branching, feedback, consequences, and reflection when they support the learning goal.

My Design Process

I connect learning needs, instructional strategy, and development throughout the design process.

01 · DISCOVER

Understand

Identify the audience, performance need, and learning goals.

02 · DESIGN

Structure

Build the learning architecture, objectives, scenarios, and storyboard.

03 · DEVELOP

Build

Turn the design into an interactive learning experience using Rise 360 and Storyline 360.

04 · REFLECT

Refine

Review the learner experience and refine the design based on what the interaction is meant to accomplish.

About Me

From classroom learning to digital learning experiences.

With a Master’s in Zoology and a Bachelor’s in Education, I combine subject-matter expertise with professional experience in teaching, curriculum development, and designing meaningful learning experiences.

My work in education has given me a strong foundation in understanding learners, structuring learning experiences, and translating complex ideas into accessible instruction.

I am now transitioning into instructional design, bringing that foundation into the design of digital and workplace learning experiences.

My portfolio focuses on scenario based learning, curriculum architecture, interactive eLearning, and thoughtful learner practice. I am particularly interested in creating learning experiences that are purposeful, practical, and connected to what learners actually need to do.
