Design Approach

My approach focuses on creating learning experiences that move beyond information delivery and give learners opportunities to practice the decisions they will need to make on the job.

Scenario-Based Learning

Learners work through realistic situations and make decisions rather than relying only on recall-based questions.

Meaningful Interaction

Interactive elements are used to create opportunities for learners to evaluate options and experience consequences.

Learning by Design

Learning objectives, instructional strategy, storyboards and development are connected throughout the design process.
